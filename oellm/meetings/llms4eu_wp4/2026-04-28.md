## Slide 4: Qualitative completions viewer
- Public web tool that **qualitatively compares model outputs** side-by-side across SFT progress, dataset mixtures (75/25 vs 25/75), and the pre-SFT base, all on the same prompt
- 12 checkpoints × 7 EU languages × 698 LMArena prompts; slider scrubs training in lockstep
- [Open the viewer](https://ferreirafabio.github.io/olmo3-multilingual-dolci-sft-progression/) [3]

---

## Slide 5: Datamix-9B Instruct SFT (OpenEuroLLM 9B alpha)
- First post-training of the OpenEuroLLM 9B alpha, same 75/25 SFT recipe as our OLMo runs [4]
- **Takeaway:** already a very strong SFT basis. Beats our OLMo SFT on most non-English at matched context, **without any context-length extension yet**. Very promising for what comes after extension
- English still lags due to the alpha's 2048-token context limit

![Per-language Elo (Datamix-9B vs OLMo, matched ctx)](../../experiments/multilingual_eu/results/plots/datamix_9b_per_language_elo_truncated.png)

## Slide 6: JudgeArena (NeurIPS submission)
- Open-source unified LLM-judge framework [5], covering AlpacaEval, Arena-Hard v0.1/v2.0, MT-Bench, m-Arena-Hard v0.1/v2.0 (23 langs)
- Tuned open-weight judges match closed-model judges in meta-eval
- New: simulated LMArena Elo from human annotations + fluency mode for base models
