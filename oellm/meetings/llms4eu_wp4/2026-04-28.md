## Slide 2 — Multilingual EU SFT
- **Dolci replay preserves English** (Elo 942–965 vs baseline 950); without replay drops to ~770
- **Sweet spot ≈ 75/25 En/EU**; pure-EU collapses both English and EU
- **Continued SFT itself causes forgetting** (100% English no-EU still drops to 791)
- Romanian transfers (Latin script); Greek does not

## Slide 3 — Dolci-Translated SFT (headline win)
- 7 EU langs, matched-compute (2.87M samples, 2 epochs)
- **A-75en**: English preserved (950 vs baseline 954), non-English 697 → 755
- **A-25en**: best non-English; Finnish **+125 Elo** over baseline; English -37
