## Slide 2 — Multilingual EU SFT: what we now know
- Full Track A–F sweep done; judged with Qwen3.5-27B (Bradley–Terry Elo, 200 battles/lang, 12 EU langs)
- **Dolci replay is the unlock**: replaying the base SFT's English data keeps English Elo at **942–965** (baseline 950) instead of collapsing to ~770 — *source* of English data matters, ratio doesn't
- **Sweet spot ≈ 75/25 En/EU** (Track F2): best overall Elo 747; pure-EU (F5) collapses both English (762) and EU (36.6% WR vs baseline)
- **Continued SFT itself causes English forgetting** (C0, 100% English no-EU still drops to 791) — not the EU data
- **Romanian transfers** (Latin script, held-out: 700–890); **Greek does not** (different script: 540–690)

## Slide 3 — Dolci-Translated SFT (high-quality gemma-3-27b translations)
- New SFT runs on top of our reproduced Instruct-SFT using **`openeurollm/Dolci-Instruct-SFT-translated`** (7 EU langs: cs, de, es, fi, fr, it, sv)
- Two ratios, **matched-compute** (2.87M samples, 2 epochs each, identical hyperparams) so the comparison isolates ratio from compute
- **A-75en preserves English perfectly**: 950±14 vs baseline 954±16 (within CI), non-English up from 697 → **755**
- **A-25en**: best non-English (cs/de/es/fi/fr all win); Finnish jumps **+125 Elo** over baseline; English costs ~37 pts
- Both ratios **beat baseline overall** → high-quality translations close the gap that NLLB couldn't
- Next: Konstantin's quality filter on the translated pool, then the Dolci-Think analogue

> *Speaker note:* This is the "headline win" — the gemma-3-27b translations deliver what the NLLB-600M ones could not.

## Slide 5 — Datamix-9B Instruct SFT (OpenEuroLLM 9B alpha)
- Ran instruct SFT on **`openeurollm/datamix-9b-80-20`** with the same dt-A-75en data, judged with Qwen3.5-27B (500 battles/lang)
- **datamix-9B beats our OLMo A-75en on 4/7 non-English at matched 1024/1024 truncation**: cs +100, de +50, fi +63, sv +34 Elo
- **Czech is the standout** (833±15 — highest non-English Elo across all our SFTs); confirms the 80/20 multilingual datamix carries through post-training
- English weaker (879 vs 970) — driven by **2048 ctx limit** (no extension yet) and alpha pretraining
- **Caveat** found while running this: Qwen3.5-27B judge slightly prefers shorter outputs → reran *all* OLMo eval at 1024/1024 for fair comparison (judge-bias note documented)

> *Speaker note:* "First evidence that EuroLLM pretraining datamix carries through post-training — Czech is striking."

## Slide 6 — JudgeArena: NeurIPS submission in progress
- Unified open-source framework: **AlpacaEval, Arena-Hard v0.1/v2.0, MT-Bench, m-Arena-Hard v0.1/v2.0** (23 langs) — single interface, swappable judges, full reproducibility metadata
- Any model on **vLLM / llama.cpp / OpenRouter** can be either contestant or judge
- Ships **multi-objective-tuned open-weight judge configs** that match or beat closed-model judges; meta-evaluated against human-preference data (English + multilingual)
- New: **simulated LMArena Elo** — combines existing human annotations with LLM-judge evaluations of a target model, approximates LMArena Elo to within ±MAE points (leave-one-out over 20 models)
- Also adds a **fluency-completion** mode (fi, fr, de, es, sv) for evaluating *base* models without chat templates
- All multilingual_eu / dolci-translated / datamix-9B numbers above were produced through this pipeline

## Q&A prep / likely questions

- **Why Qwen3.5-27B over Qwen3-30B-A3B as judge?** Dense model, more stable LMArena-Elo correlation in our meta-eval; ran both for some tables during the migration.
- **Why is datamix-9B's English worse?** 2048 ctx limit (no extension yet), and the base is an early alpha pretrained on only 4B tokens.
- **Why does 1024/1024 truncation flip some comparisons?** Qwen3.5-27B judge has a mild brevity preference — documented in `datamix_9b_sft_results.md`. Mitigation: report both regimes when comparing across context limits.
- **What's next on Dolci-Translated?** Apply Konstantin's Propella quality filter to the translated pool; then run the Dolci-**Think** analogue once translations land.
- **Can partners use the qualitative viewer?** Yes — public, no auth, works on mobile, HF Space mirror in case GitHub Pages is blocked.
