## Slide 2 — Multilingual EU SFT: what we learned
- **Replaying the base SFT's English data** is the unlock: keeps English Elo at the baseline level (~950) instead of collapsing to ~770
- **Sweet spot ≈ 75/25 English/EU** for overall Elo across 12 EU langs; pure-EU collapses both English *and* EU
- **Continued SFT itself causes English forgetting** — even a 100% English continued-SFT (no EU at all) drops English Elo
- **Cross-lingual transfer**: Romanian (held-out, Latin script) transfers cleanly; Greek (different script) does not
- Net: the *source* of the English replay matters more than the exact ratio

## Slide 3 — Dolci-Translated SFT (the headline win)
- New translated data: `openeurollm/Dolci-Instruct-SFT-translated` — **gemma-3-27b-it** translations of Dolci into 7 EU langs (cs, de, es, fi, fr, it, sv)
- Two ratios run **at matched compute** (same 2.87M samples × 2 epochs, identical hyperparams) so we isolate ratio from compute:
  - **75% English / 25% translated** preserves English perfectly (within CI of baseline) and lifts non-English Elo from 697 → 755
  - **25% English / 75% translated** wins on most non-English; **Finnish +125 Elo** over baseline; English costs ~37 pts
- High-quality MT closes the gap NLLB-distilled-600M couldn't — translation quality was the bottleneck
- Next: Konstantin's quality filter on the translated pool, then the Think-SFT analogue

## Slide 5 — Datamix-9B Instruct SFT (OpenEuroLLM 9B alpha)
- First post-training of `openeurollm/datamix-9b-80-20` (Llama arch, Gemma tokenizer, 4B-token 80/20 multilingual pretraining)
- Same recipe as OLMo's 75/25-en mix, judged with Qwen3.5-27B (500 battles/lang)
- **Beats our OLMo 75/25 on 4/7 non-English** at fair 1024/1024 truncation: cs +100, de +50, fi +63, sv +34 Elo
- **Czech 833±15** — highest non-English Elo across all our SFTs → multilingual pretraining datamix carries through
- English weaker (2048 ctx limit, alpha pretraining); judge has mild brevity bias — reran OLMo at matched ctx for fairness

## Slide 6 — JudgeArena (NeurIPS)
- Open-source unified framework for LLM-judge evaluation
- Benchmarks: AlpacaEval, Arena-Hard v0.1/v2.0, MT-Bench, **m-Arena-Hard v0.1/v2.0** (23 langs)
- Any vLLM / llama.cpp / OpenRouter model can be **either contestant or judge** — single interface, full reproducibility metadata
- Tuned **open-weight judges** match closed-model judges in meta-eval against human-preference data (EN + multilingual)
- New: **simulated LMArena Elo** by combining existing human annotations with LLM-judge eval of a target model — within ±MAE pts of LMArena (LOO over 20 models)
- Adds **fluency-completion mode** for evaluating *base* models (no chat template) in fi, fr, de, es, sv
- All numbers in this update were produced through this pipeline
